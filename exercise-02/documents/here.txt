Company Handbook: Nimbus Robotics

1. Company Overview

Nimbus Robotics was founded in 2016 in Austin, Texas. The company builds
autonomous warehouse robots used for inventory scanning and package sorting.
As of 2026, Nimbus Robotics has around 340 employees and operates in six
countries: the United States, Canada, Germany, Japan, Brazil, and Australia.
The company's flagship product line is called the Scout series, which
includes the Scout Mini, Scout Standard, and Scout Heavy models.

2. Products

The Scout Mini is a compact robot designed for small warehouses under
10,000 square feet. It has a battery life of approximately 6 hours and can
carry loads up to 15 kilograms. The Scout Standard is the most widely sold
model, with a battery life of 10 hours and a load capacity of 40 kilograms.
The Scout Heavy is designed for industrial distribution centers, with a
battery life of 8 hours and a load capacity of 150 kilograms. All Scout
robots use LIDAR-based navigation and can operate without pre-mapped
facility layouts.

3. Remote Work Policy

Employees at Nimbus Robotics may work remotely up to three days per week,
subject to manager approval. Engineering and design teams are generally
granted more flexibility than roles that require on-site hardware testing.
Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable during core hours,
defined as 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time. Fully remote arrangements are
only approved on a case-by-case basis and require VP-level sign-off.

4. Vacation and Leave

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation per year, in addition
to 10 paid holidays. New employees begin accruing vacation immediately but
cannot use accrued days until after their first 90 days of employment.
Parental leave is offered at 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver
and 8 weeks fully paid for a secondary caregiver, regardless of gender.

5. Expense Reimbursement

Employees may submit expense reports for approved business travel, client
meetings, and conference attendance. Reimbursement requests must be
submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Any single expense
over $500 requires prior written approval from a director-level manager or
above. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day.

6. Customer Support

Nimbus Robotics offers three support tiers: Standard, Priority, and
Enterprise. Standard support includes email support with a 48-hour response
window. Priority support adds phone support and a 4-hour response window
during business hours. Enterprise support includes a dedicated account
manager, 24/7 phone support, and an on-site technician visit within 48
hours for critical hardware failures.

7. Safety Certifications

All Scout robots are certified under ISO 3691-4, the international
standard for safety requirements of driverless industrial trucks. The
Scout Heavy additionally holds CE certification for use in the European
Union. Nimbus Robotics conducts internal safety audits every quarter and
publishes an annual safety report on its website.
